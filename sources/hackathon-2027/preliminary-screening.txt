REVIEWER NOTES

Scoring & source basis

An MSRC adaptation of published screening approaches; equal weights are proposed, not externally validated.
ONE CONSISTENT 1-5 SCALE

5 Excellent                         Both guiding questions convincingly addressed; strong support.
4 Strong                            Both addressed well; only minor gaps.
3 Adequate                          Core expectations met, but some development is needed.
2 Weak                              Substantial gaps in explanation, justification or practicality.
1 Poor                              Criterion largely unaddressed or unsupported.

HOW THE MSRC CRITERIA WERE DERIVED
01 Problem Relevance & Potential Impact
Taiwan: Social Impact | EPRA: Relevance + Societal Impact
Combines importance of the problem, track fit and plausible benefit.

02 Innovation & Solution Fit

Taiwan: Innovation + Feasibility | EPRA: Innovation
Pairs originality with whether the solution actually addresses the need.

03 Feasibility & Development Potential

Taiwan: Feasibility | UKRI/NQCC: Hackathon Suitability
Judges a realistic path forward, not a completed product or prototype.

04 Evidence & Rationale

EPRA: Use of Data & Analytical Approach | UKRI/NQCC: Supporting Information
Retained independently because MSRC is a research-focused conference.

REVIEW PROTOCOL

Eligibility first: confirm submission completeness, team/track rules, originality declarations and any safety or ethics concerns before
competitive scoring.

Independent scoring: two blinded reviewers score each eligible application out of 20. Average reviewer totals and rank within each track;
define a third-review process for close or disputed cases.

Fairness: score the substantive content of the summary, deck and optional material. Do not separately reward polished design, videos,
previous progress or a prototype.
