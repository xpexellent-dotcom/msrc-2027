## Direction contract

THESIS: A cinematic MSRC conference experience with expansive photography,
left-aligned display typography and a single transforming navigation system.

OWN-WORLD: MSRC purple, gold, ivory and deep ink; large Manrope and Noto Sans Arabic,
softly rounded photographic panels, fine rules and restrained translucent navigation.

STORY: Recognize the congress, see the date, explore five participation pathways,
preview the programme and people, then discover the previous edition and venue.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Edge-to-edge approved video at full viewport height. A light-weight,
oversized title sits at the left reading margin; supporting facts and actions sit
below. A distinctive large countdown occupies the lower right, stacking on phones.
The floating navigation separates into two islands while reading and expands on
pointer approach or keyboard focus. Arabic mirrors the reading composition.

FORM: User-pinned Axiom Power reference applied to the existing MSRC identity;
no new random direction assignment. Existing seed e18ccd6f is historical only.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## Interaction and content constraints

- Programme dropdown: Day 1 / Day 2 URLs. Participation: Attend, Research,
  Workshops, Hackathon, postgraduate 3MT. Our Team replaces Media in the header.
- Logo uses normal home navigation/reload. Media remains discoverable from the
  MSRC 2026 section and footer. New team/sponsor pages use honest demo states.
- Sponsor marquee flows left-to-right, can be paused, and is static for reduced
  motion. Carousels remain native scroll areas with visible arrow controls.
- All pointer interactions have keyboard/touch equivalents. Reduced motion removes
  travel; native scrolling remains. No fake roster, schedule, tiers-as-approved,
  sponsor affiliation or registration availability. "Join MSRC" can lead to the
  closed registration information page.
- Drive photographs are authorized for this local approval demo. Preserve originals
  and record provenance. Jeddah footage needs an identified usable source; never
  label unrelated footage as Jeddah. No production publication is authorized.
