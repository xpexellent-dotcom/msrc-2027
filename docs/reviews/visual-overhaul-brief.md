# Public design demo — 11 October 2026

The organizer delegates the visual direction and requests a complete working demo
for approval. This expands the preceding audit and refinement scope. Implementation
is code-led so the approval artifact includes real bilingual content, filters,
responsive layouts and film behavior. No release is authorized by this brief.

## Prioritized plan

1. Retain the preceding accessibility, enlarged-text, keyboard, URL-state and film fixes.
2. Replace the public page composition and repeated card system with a coherent
   congress-poster identity. Carry the same grid, typography, controls and surfaces
   through the homepage and public interiors.
3. Calm decorative motion, retain purposeful state feedback and native scrolling.
4. Verify EN/AR, small and large screens, motion preferences, real interactions and
   closed workflow boundaries; supply a running demo and evidence for approval.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A contemporary scientific congress poster brought to the web. Bold
typography and an editorial programme index replace repeated illustration cards.

OWN-WORLD: MSRC purple, gold, ivory and ink; Manrope display type and Inter text,
with Noto Sans Arabic. Broad color planes, thin rules, generous space, open
editorial rows and restrained rectangular controls form one recognizable system.

STORY: The visitor recognizes a credible student research congress, sees the
approved date and setting, then compares participation routes and programme status.
The previous-edition film is the authentic proof; unpublished content stays honest.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A full-width ivory masthead above a full-height cinematic poster.
A large two-line title sits low on the film with a slim conference identity line,
readable supporting text and a paired action row. A large outlined edition motif
occupies the opposite side only where space permits. Below, a gold event-information
rail leads into a spacious editorial introduction. On phones the title, actions
and metadata become a single natural column without decorative competition.

FORM: International typographic congress poster, seventh of seven independently
grounded candidates; concept-seed key e18ccd6f. Other candidates were scientific
journal, wayfinding, exhibition catalogue, peer-review marginalia, auditorium
programme and academic archive. User delegated selection and approval follows demo.

MOTION: The signature interaction is the existing same-video cinema expansion.
Typography enters once, with a calmer reversible scroll treatment. Routine pointer
feedback lasts 180–220ms; keyboard movement is immediate. Reduced motion is static.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## Challenger evaluation

All six seeded challengers lose audience identification and product clarity to the
assigned congress-poster direction; none becomes the build candidate. Their donated
discipline raises the chosen direction without importing their visual motifs:

- Technical magazine: total commitment to the MSRC palette across whole sections.
- Portrait plate: disciplined image provenance and clear caption hierarchy.
- Shader portal: an immersive opening, using the approved film and native scrolling.
- Darkroom: one deliberate media transition rather than competing motion effects.
- Paper automata: responsive composition designed as carefully as the desktop.
- Vector field: one dominant focal point in each section, without decorative clutter.

## Constraints

Preserve approved public facts and policy text, EN/AR RTL parity, native controls,
filter URLs, 44px touch targets, focus, reduced motion and no-JS readability. The
only conference photography is the cleared previous-edition hero media. Operational
workflows, staff interfaces and deployment settings are outside this demo scope.
